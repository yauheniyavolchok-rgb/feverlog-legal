⚠️ Fraud warning

We will never contact you first to ask for personal information, your password, or any kind of payment. If you receive a message claiming to be from FeverLog asking for any of these, it is not from us.

Questions, bug reports, or data requests

Email developer01db@gmail.com and describe what's going on — what you expected, what happened instead, and your device's iOS version if it's a bug report. We read every message.

Deleting your data

You can leave a household, delete a household you own, permanently delete a child's records, or delete your entire account at any time from Settings inside the app — no need to email us for this. See the Privacy Policy for details.
