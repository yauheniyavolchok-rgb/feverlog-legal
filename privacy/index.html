Privacy Policy

FeverLog for iOS · Last updated October 2026

⚠️ Fraud warning

Nobody developing or supporting FeverLog will ever contact you to ask for personal information, your password, or any kind of payment. If you receive a message claiming to be from FeverLog asking for any of these, it is not from us — do not respond, do not share anything, and do not pay. Questions about the app are welcome at the contact above, but we will never initiate contact asking you for information or money.

Who's responsible for your data

Y.Vau develops and operates FeverLog and is the data controller for any information you choose to sync. For privacy questions, data access requests, or help with deletion, contact developer01db@gmail.com. You can also delete your own data directly in the app at any time — see “Deleting your data” below.

We ask for as little as possible

FeverLog is built to need as little personal information as possible. We never ask for your name, your child's real name, a home address, a phone number, or a birthdate. For your child's profile, feel free to use a nickname instead of a real name — “Bunny” or “Sunshine” works just as well, and the app behaves exactly the same either way. Anything personal you choose to type into a name or note field is there because you put it there, not because the app asked for it — that choice, and the responsibility for it, is yours.

Where your data lives

Temperature readings, medication logs, symptoms, and notes are stored locally on this device using SwiftData. Nothing leaves the device unless household sync is turned on.

Household sync

Household sync is optional. When enabled, your records are copied to Supabase so the people in your household can see the same log. You can use FeverLog fully offline without ever turning this on.

How long we keep your data

By turning on household sync, you agree that your records are kept for up to one year. Any individual health record — a temperature reading, a dose, a symptom, a note — older than one year is automatically and permanently deleted on an ongoing basis, not just once a year. If a household sees no activity at all for a full year (no change to the household, any child in it, or any record), the entire household and everything in it is automatically and permanently deleted. You don't have to wait for either of these — you can delete any of it yourself, at any time, from Settings. See “Deleting your data” below.

Deleting your data

You can leave a household, delete a household you own, permanently delete a child's records, or delete your entire account at any time from Settings. These actions are immediate and irreversible: there is no backup, and deleted data cannot be recovered — not by you, and not by us.

Your responsibility

If you invite someone to your household with an invite code, you're responsible for who you share that code with — anyone who redeems it gets full access to your household's data. Likewise, anything personal you choose to type into the app — a real name, identifying details in a note — is entered at your own discretion and is your responsibility, not the app's.

Security measures

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, and will keep this list current if that changes:

  • All data sent to our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Server-side access rules ensure only the members of your own household can ever see that household's data.
  • Sensitive actions — creating a household, generating invite codes, deleting data — are enforced by server-side checks, not just by the app itself.
  • Household invite codes expire after 7 days and can only be used once.
  • Data stored on your device is set to the strongest level of your device's built-in encryption — unreadable the instant your device is locked, not just until it's first unlocked after a restart.
  • Our backend provider (Supabase) is responsible for the security of its own infrastructure, including encryption of data at rest.

No analytics, no tracking

FeverLog does not collect usage analytics or send your data to any third party.
